CORTIS: A Rising Star

CORTIS is a South Korean boy group under BIGHIT MUSIC. Since their debut, they have gained international attention through their distinctive music, creative performances, and growing global fanbase.

Their remarkable achievements have established them as one of the notable new-generationK-pop groups.

A Collection of Achievements

CORTIS has received several prestigious recognitions, including Rookie of the Yearat the 2025 Asia Artist Awards and Best New Artist at the 2025 MAMA Awards.

They also received the Rookie Artist of the Year award at the 2026 Golden Disc Awards and Best K-pop New Artist at the 2026 iHeartRadio Music Awards.
